# 100-Day Fast Conversational Tamil Plan

## Goal

Become understandable in everyday spoken Tamil as quickly as possible.

You are **not** trying to read/write Tamil script, speak perfectly, or study formal Tamil. You are trying to understand common spoken Tamil, ask/answer questions, communicate basic needs, and expand sentences by plugging vocabulary into reusable patterns.

Everything here uses **English transliteration only**.

## Daily Method

Every day learn:

- **2 sentence patterns**
- **3 new words**

For each pattern:

1. Understand the meaning.
2. Say it aloud at least 5 times.
3. Substitute different words into it.
4. Answer questions using it.
5. Use it in a tiny conversation.

Spend **15–20 minutes/day**.

When you ask ChatGPT `Day 17`, it should teach only that day first, then review yesterday, teach today's patterns and words, make you construct sentences, ask questions, do a short conversation, and correct important mistakes.

---

## Pronunciation Notes

- `aa` = long “a” sound, as in “father”.
- `th` is a soft dental `t`, not the English “th” in “think”.
- `zh` is the Tamil retroflex sound in words like `tamizh`; approximate it gently if hard.
- Spoken Tamil often shortens words. This plan prioritizes understandable spoken Tamil.

## How to Use Each Day With ChatGPT

Use this prompt:

> Day __. Teach me only today's material first. Review yesterday's patterns, teach today's two patterns and three words, make me construct sentences, ask me questions, do a short conversation, and correct important mistakes. Do not use Tamil script.

## Milestones

- **Days 1–10:** survival needs, basic identity, requests.
- **Days 11–30:** daily actions, present/past/future, understanding.
- **Days 31–50:** people, feelings, places, food, directions.
- **Days 51–70:** reasons, conditions, phone, meetings, politeness.
- **Days 71–90:** learning Tamil, home/work, numbers, opinions, problems.
- **Days 91–100:** fluency connectors, storytelling, small talk, integration.
